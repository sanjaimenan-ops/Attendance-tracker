2. Calculation settings

12-hour clock only: enter AM/PM (for example IN 10:00 AM, OUT 09:30 PM = 11.50 clocked hours). Do not enter bare times without AM/PM. Rules: net paid hours = clocked hours − lunch time exceeding the included lunch allowance − permission deduction. Regular hours are capped at shift hours; remaining hours are overtime. Missing lunch punches create no extra lunch deduction. Paid leave defaults to 0 hours until you choose otherwise.

How privacy works

This prototype processes the TXT file inside your browser. Attendance records are saved in this browser's local storage; they are not uploaded to a server. Use a personal device and avoid clearing browser data if you want to retain the records. This is not yet a hosted, password-protected website and does not sync between devices.
